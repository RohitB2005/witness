# Harbour Clean — booking app, fixed scope

*Proposal agreed with Harbour Clean Ltd (fictional). This is the scope a client signs off; it's used as
Witness's `--requirements` input in the benchmark.*

Harbour Clean is a home and office cleaning company in Auckland. The web app lets customers see what
we offer, get a price, book a clean, and manage their bookings. Prices are shown excluding GST; GST is
15%.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Customers can browse all services, each with its price.
2. Customers can search services by name.
3. Customers can sort services by price, from low to high and from high to low.
4. The quote calculator shows a subtotal, the GST at 15%, and a total including GST.
5. Customers can book a clean for a future date, with or without an account. Past dates are refused.
6. After booking, the customer sees a confirmation with a booking reference.
7. Customers can log in and see a list of their upcoming bookings.
8. Customers can cancel an existing booking.
9. Customers can reschedule an existing booking to a new date and time.
10. Customers can update their name and phone number on their profile, and the change is kept.
11. Customers can send us a message through the contact form.

## Out of scope

Online payment, staff rostering, and a mobile app.
